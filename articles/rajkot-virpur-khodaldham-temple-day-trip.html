Rajkot to Virpur and Khodaldham: Temple Day Trip and Visitor Guide

Plan a visit to Jalaram Mandir in Virpur and Khodaldham in Kagvad using official distance, transport and contact information.

Virpur and Kagvad are two distinct pilgrimage destinations south of Rajkot. A visit can combine the shrine associated with Jalaram Bapa in Virpur with the temple dedicated to Goddess Khodal in Kagvad. Both are active places of worship, so plan around devotional activities and respect the customs observed at each site.

Jalaram Mandir, Virpur

Gujarat Tourism identifies Virpur as the birthplace of Jalaram Bapa, a Hindu saint born in 1799 and devoted to Rama. The shrine occupies the house complex associated with his life. Visitors can learn about the saint through objects connected with his religious tradition and the deities worshipped there. Confirm the current darshan schedule and any restrictions on photography before visiting. Do not assume that facilities or arrangements described in older travel blogs remain unchanged.

Shree Khodaldham, Kagvad

The Khodaldham Trust describes its temple as a sculptural religious complex in Kagvad village, Jetpur taluka. The campus information mentions gardens, shaded gazebos and Shaktivan. It also provides separate advance-booking information for specific religious offerings and ceremonies. These services should not be confused with ordinary visitor admission.

Suggested day plan

Begin with Virpur if its confirmed worship schedule suits your arrival. Allow a break before travelling onward to Kagvad. Spend the remaining time at Khodaldham and return to Rajkot without rushing. During festivals, allow for queues and changes to traffic or visitor arrangements. Reversing the order may be more convenient depending on the day's timetable.

Darshan timings and visitor etiquette

The official sources reviewed do not establish a definitive current daily darshan schedule for both temples. Contact the respective temple before travel. Wear clothing appropriate to a place of worship, follow footwear and photography instructions, and respect queues. Ask about accessibility, food service and restroom facilities rather than assuming they are available at every hour.

The Khodaldham Trust lists its temple contact as 02823-252000 and info@khodaldhamtrust.org. Its official site also provides live darshan and booking information.
