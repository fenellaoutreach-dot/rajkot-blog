Jetalsar Junction's ₹17 Crore Upgrade: New Bridge and Passenger Facilities

Jetalsar Junction, a railway station in Rajkot district, is being redeveloped under the Amrit Bharat Station Scheme, with an estimated ₹17 crore programme reported by Gujarat Samachar on 22 November 2026. The work includes improved platform access, passenger information and station facilities. Some improvements are operating already; others remain under construction.

What is changing at Jetalsar?

According to Gujarat Samachar's 22 November report, the project was approved on 27 March 2026. Plans include redesigned access and parking areas, platform shelters, separate toilet facilities, a Government Railway Police office and an electronic coach guidance system.

The newspaper reports that an extension of the existing foot overbridge linking Platform 1 with Platforms 4 and 5 opened on 16 March 2026. A further bridge, described as 159 metres long and 6 metres wide, is under construction at an estimated ₹13.42 crore. The report says lifts and accessible features are intended across the platforms, while a lift serving Platforms 2 and 3 is already operating.

Water, train information and emergency facilities

Gujarat Samachar also reports that a 2.25 lakh-litre overhead water tank entered service on 31 March 2026, and an Integrated Passenger Information System began operating in July 2026. A road mobile restoration vehicle was deployed in June, according to the same report. These are reported project milestones, not independently inspected facilities.

Why this matters to passengers

Wider foot overbridges, lifts and clearer train information could make station transfers easier, particularly for passengers with mobility needs or luggage. However, travellers should not assume the proposed new bridge and all amenities are finished. No verified final completion date was provided in the reporting reviewed for this article.

The Government of India's Press Information Bureau independently lists Jetalsar Junction among Western Railway stations identified under the Amrit Bharat Station Scheme. That official list confirms inclusion in the programme; the detailed costs, dates and work status above are attributed to Gujarat Samachar.

Sources and verification

Primary government programme confirmation: Press Information Bureau, Ministry of Railways. Project status and figures: Gujarat Samachar, 22 November 2026. This is an independently written summary of attributed reporting, not an on-site inspection or a railway press statement.
