Rotary Dolls Museum Rajkot: A Family Guide to 1,600+ Dolls from Around the World (2026)

A museum in central Rajkot uses dolls to introduce visitors to the clothing, traditions and cultures of places far beyond Gujarat. The Rotary Dolls Museum is a distinctive indoor stop for families, students and curious travellers.

LocationDr Yagnik Road, Rajkot
Official website hoursTue–Sun, 10 AM–7 PM
Weekly closureMonday

Why the collection stands out

According to the museum's own introduction, the collection contains more than 1,600 ethnic and traditional dolls from over 102 countries. The project was conceived in 2001 by Rotarian Deepak Agrawal and is associated with the Rotary Club of Rajkot Midtown. These are the museum's published figures, not an independent inventory conducted by Rajkot.blog.

The Rajkot district administration describes a bi-level museum where dolls in traditional dress introduce visitors to cultures around the world. Rather than treating the displays as toys alone, visitors can look at the details of clothing, decoration and presentation as a starting point for conversations about geography and heritage.

What families and students can do here

For children, try a simple cultural observation activity: choose a few exhibits, read their labels and compare the materials, colours and styles represented. Ask which details might relate to climate, celebrations or regional traditions, while remembering that a single costume cannot represent everyone in a country. Older visitors may enjoy discussing how clothing changes across regions and generations.

For schools, the museum could provide a starting point for a supervised lesson on world cultures. Any group visit, educational activity or photography permission should be arranged directly with the museum; Rajkot.blog has not confirmed a current school programme.

Where is the Rotary Dolls Museum?

The district tourism listing places it on the second floor of Nagrik Bank Building, Yagnik Road, opposite Heera Panna Complex, Jagnath Plot, Rajkot. The museum's contact page also identifies Dr Yagnik Road, opposite Heera Panna Shopping Complex. Confirm the entrance and accessibility arrangements before travelling, especially if you are visiting with a stroller or someone who needs step-free access.

Opening hours, Monday closure and tickets

The official museum contact page currently states Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM, with the ticket window closing half an hour earlier, and closure on Mondays. The Rajkot Municipal Corporation's tourism booklet publishes a different split-hour schedule and an entry fee of ₹30. Because those official sources do not agree on hours, do not assume the booklet's times or fee remain current. Call 0281-2464352 to confirm today's opening hours, admission, camera policy and any holiday changes.

Planning a relaxed visit

Allow roughly an hour as a flexible personal planning estimate, not a museum-stated visit duration. Consider combining the visit with other central Rajkot attractions if their opening hours suit your day. If you are visiting with children, encourage them to choose a few countries to learn about rather than rushing through every display. Ask staff before photographing exhibits.

How this connects to Rajkot's cultural economy

Small cultural attractions can complement local cafés, transport services and other visitor experiences. The museum's international theme also gives Rajkot families a way to explore cultural diversity without leaving the city.
