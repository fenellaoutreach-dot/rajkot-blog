Famous Things to Buy in Rajkot: Bandhani, Crafts, Jewellery & Food Gifts

Rajkot.blog editorial team · 28 November 2026

Useful things to buy in Rajkot include bandhani textiles, bangles, embroidered fabrics, silver jewellery, carved wooden or metal crafts and regional food gifts such as penda and farsan. Rather than listing market locations again, this guide focuses on what to inspect before paying and how to take purchases home.

Bandhani and embroidered fabrics

Bandhani uses tied sections of cloth to create patterned dye effects. Ask whether a piece is hand-tied, printed or made using another technique, and what fabric it uses. Do not rely on a seller's verbal claim alone for expensive purchases. Check the edges and colour-care instructions before buying a sari, dupatta or dress material. Rajkot's district administration documents a regional tradition of textile printing and embroidery. See the Rashtriya Shala handloom guide for local heritage context.

Bangles, silver jewellery and decorative craft

Choose bangles by fit and comfort, especially when buying gifts for someone who is not present. Ask for an exchange policy. For silver or gold items, request a written description of metal purity, weight and charges. The district handicrafts page also documents metalwork, wood carving and wood with metal embossing. For decorative items, inspect joints, edges and the underside, and check whether the seller can package fragile goods securely.

Food souvenirs: penda and farsan

Regional sweets and savoury snacks make convenient gifts only when storage and transport suit the product. Ask the shop about preparation date and refrigeration requirements for sweets. Check sealed snack labels for ingredients and expiry information. See our penda buying guide and farsan shopping guide for details.

What to buy for different travel needs

For a small carry bag, consider a lightweight dupatta, a small craft or sealed snacks. For an expensive textile or jewellery purchase, allow time to compare sellers and obtain a receipt. For fragile objects, ask about packing and transport rather than assuming every item fits in luggage. A gift sold in Rajkot is not necessarily made there: ask about origin if local production matters to you.

Where to shop

Gujarat Tourism and IndiGo's Rajkot guide document Bangdi Bazaar's textile and bangle shopping. Our local market directory compares Bangdi Bazaar, Dharmendra Road, Soni Bazaar and Mochi Bazaar by shopping purpose. This article does not claim current individual shop stock or prices.

FAQs

What is Rajkot famous for shopping?

Regional textiles, bangles, jewellery and traditional crafts are documented shopping interests; food gifts are another option.

Is every bandhani item locally handmade?

No. Verify technique and origin with the seller, especially for expensive purchases.
