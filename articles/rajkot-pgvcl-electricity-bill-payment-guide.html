PGVCL Electricity Bill in Rajkot: Online Payment and Complaint Guide

Check your PGVCL consumer account, use the official Gujarat electricity service portal, retain receipts and find the utility's published customer-care channels.

Electricity bills for PGVCL consumers in Rajkot are separate from municipal property tax and water charges. Begin with the supplier printed on your latest electricity bill. Paschim Gujarat Vij Company Limited publishes its corporate contact details in Rajkot and participates in Gujarat's common online electricity services portal.

Before paying your electricity bill

Locate the consumer number on the bill, identify the supply address and review the billing period and amount due. If you have recently moved or live in rented accommodation, confirm whose account is being paid and whether the balance relates to earlier occupancy. Do not use an unfamiliar payment link sent by an unsolicited message.

Where to check and pay PGVCL bills

  1. Start from PGVCL's official website or the GUVNL electricity services portal.
  2. Select the payment or bill service for your supplier and follow its current consumer-identification requirements.
  3. Compare the account and amount with the bill. Check whether earlier dues are included.
  4. Complete the transaction through the official service and save its acknowledgement. Retain both the bank reference and utility receipt.

The published GUVNL service menu includes bill download, consumption history, past payments and service requests. The portal did not load successfully during our direct check, so this guide identifies the published official entry points rather than claiming to have tested a payment.

Understand a higher-than-usual bill

Compare consumption units, billing dates, earlier balances and adjustments with previous bills. Consider whether appliance use or occupancy has changed. If the meter reading or account details appear incorrect, record the information visible on your bill and contact the supplier; never open or interfere with electrical equipment.

PGVCL customer care and grievances

PGVCL's official contact page lists toll-free customer-care numbers 19122 and 1800 233 155333. Save a complaint reference and any response. If the matter remains unresolved, the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission publishes a consumer grievance forum directory, including the Rajkot PGVCL forum. Confirm the current procedure and eligibility before escalating.

Keep bill and service requests separate

The common portal lists requests for new connections, changes to name, load or category, and power supply complaints. Paying a bill does not itself complete any of those processes. For domestic solar applications see our rooftop solar guide; for municipal bills see property tax and water charges.
