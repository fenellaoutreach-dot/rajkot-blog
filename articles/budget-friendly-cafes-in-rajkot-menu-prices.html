Sample menu prices and a two-person order

The Drive In Cinema listing shows two cups of filter coffee at ₹180, four samosas at ₹99 and dhokla at ₹120. Combining the coffee and samosas gives an illustrative ₹279 total, or ₹139.50 per person, before any additional charges. This is an arithmetic example based on the listing, not a fixed combo or a promised price. The Classic Sports Arena listing also shows two filter coffees at ₹180 and four samosas at ₹99.

Other menu options to compare

Basil Valley in Mota Mava publishes a vegetarian menu featuring fries, sandwiches, chaat and gathiya. The accessible menu did not establish current prices for these items, so we do not label it cheaper than Tea Post. Ask for a current price list if considering it. For a bigger meal, compare the unlimited thali guide instead of ordering many individual café snacks.

Make a budget before ordering

  • Set a spending limit for drinks, food and any transport.
  • Compare quantity, not just menu item names: a two-cup serving differs from one cup.
  • Ask whether advertised prices include taxes, packaging or service charges.
  • Compare a current dine-in menu against delivery prices; discounts may apply only to particular orders.
  • Choose a branch near your route so transport does not exceed your food savings.
