6 Cafes in Rajkot: Locations, Menus & Opening Hours

Updated 28 November 2026 · Rajkot.blog editorial team

Looking for a cafe in Rajkot? Compare six options: Basil Valley, White Valley, Jasper, SARAZA, Rivarc and Tea Post. Mota Mava is a useful starting area, especially around Kalavad Road and Second Ring Road. This independent shortlist uses published business information rather than an untested ranking.

Basil Valley Café

Basil Valley describes itself as a vegetarian café offering handcrafted coffee, pastries and light meals. Its official website lists Kalavad Road, opposite Rangoli Park, near Grace College in Mota Mava. Official website.

Published opening hours: Monday–Thursday 10 AM–midnight; Friday 10 AM–1 AM; Saturday 10 AM–1:30 AM; Sunday 4 PM–1 AM (closing after midnight). Source: Basil Valley visit page, checked 28 November 2026. Confirm holiday hours directly.

White Valley Café & Multicuisine Restaurant

White Valley promotes vegetarian Italian, Continental and North Indian food alongside poolside dining and private-event facilities. Its website places it on Dharamshi Korat Road, opposite Knockout Game Zone, Mota Mava. Official website.

Jasper Café, Restaurant and Party Lawns

Jasper combines café drinks with a broad menu of snacks, pizzas and several cuisines. Its official site lists Patel Chowk, near Aqua Coral, Second Ring Road, Mota Mava, and also promotes event space. Official website.

Its published menu includes hot and cold drinks, sandwiches, pizza and full meals. Check the current menu and event terms directly.

SARAZA

SARAZA publishes links to its café and dine-in menus, as well as multiple bakery and café outlets. Its official Linktree includes Raiya Road, Speedwell Road and Nagrik Bank Chowk outlet references; confirm which outlet fits your plans. Official menu and outlet links.

Rivarc Multicuisine Restaurant & Café

Rivarc says it opened in March 2025 and describes indoor, rooftop and garden dining spaces, plus Italian, Mexican, Punjabi and Chinese food. Check its website for the current location and booking details. Official website.

Tea Post – The Desi Café

Tea Post has a listed location near Drive In Cinema in the Rajkot/Vajdi area and is positioned around tea, coffee and snacks. Check the specific outlet's menu and hours before travelling.

How to choose the right café

For a casual coffee, check beverage options and seating; for a family meal, compare the food menu; for celebrations, confirm group seating, reservation rules and any event charges. Accessibility, parking, opening hours and dietary needs are worth checking directly.

Planning a café visit by purpose

Coffee or dessert: compare the drinks and dessert sections of the actual branch menu, including any service charges. Family outing: ask about indoor seating, high chairs where needed and vegetarian choices. Group gathering: confirm table capacity, advance booking and any event package minimums. Working or studying: ask about Wi-Fi, sockets and laptop-use rules instead of assuming every café welcomes long stays.
