Mahatma Gandhi Museum Rajkot: Timings, Tickets & Visitor Guide (2026)

Planning a history-focused outing in Rajkot? Here is a practical guide to the Mahatma Gandhi Museum, based on the museum's own published visitor information.

What is the Mahatma Gandhi Museum?

The museum presents Gandhi's journey from Mohandas to Mahatma through galleries covering life events and multimedia displays, including 3D projection. It is a useful stop for visitors interested in the history of Gandhi and Rajkot. Source: official museum website.

Opening hours and entry cutoff

The museum publishes opening hours of 10 AM to 7 PM, with Monday closed. Ticket booking is listed as 10 AM to 6 PM, and the venue says museum visitors must enter before 6 PM even if they already have a ticket. Check for holiday or operational changes before travelling. Official timings.

Ticket prices listed by the museum

The museum homepage currently lists admission at ₹10 for children aged 3–12 and ₹25 for visitors over 12, with a foreign-visitor category listed at ₹400. It separately lists light-and-sound show prices of ₹10 for children aged 3–12 and ₹25 for visitors over 12. Confirm the current applicable price at the venue before paying. Official published ticket chart.

Light-and-sound show: check ticket rules first

The museum's timings page lists the show at 7:30–7:50 PM. However, its own pages disagree on whether a museum admission ticket includes the show: the timings page says yes for the same date, while the homepage says a separate ticket is required. We cannot verify which rule currently applies. Ask the museum directly before relying on either statement. Both pages say show-only tickets must be bought before 6 PM. Timings page · Homepage and price chart.

Where is the museum?

The published address is Jubilee Chowk, Jawahar Road, Rajkot 360001, Gujarat, India. The museum lists +91 9712347638 for enquiries. Official contact details.

Practical visitor checklist

Arrive with enough time to explore the galleries before the 6 PM admission cutoff. If you hope to attend the evening show, verify its availability and whether an extra ticket is needed. Confirm accessibility, photography rules, parking arrangements and any special closure directly with the museum. No first-hand visit is claimed in this guide.

Frequently asked questions

Is it open on Monday? The published schedule says no.

When does museum entry stop? Before 6 PM, according to the operator.

Is the evening show included? The museum's own pages conflict, so call to confirm.
