Rajkot Farsan Guide: Ganthiya, Chevdo and Snacks to Take Home

Learn the differences between Gujarati farsan snacks and how to compare fresh and packaged options in Rajkot.

Farsan is a useful word to learn before shopping for snacks in Rajkot. It describes a family of savoury Gujarati preparations rather than one particular dish. Some are served fresh with tea or as part of a meal; others are sold in sealed packets to take home. Knowing which texture and ingredients you want makes a snack-shop visit much easier.

Ganthiya, sev and chevdo: what is the difference?

Gujarat Tourism describes ganthiya as a fried chickpea-flour snack with a notably soft, airy texture. Ganthiya is also written gathiya or ganthia on English menus. It is not the same as thin sev, which is often crisp and used as a topping for savoury mixtures. Chevdo is a mixture rather than a single shaped snack; ingredients can include flattened rice, sev, nuts and spices, depending on the recipe.

GanthiyaPlain or seasoned; freshly prepared or packaged

What does a local shop actually sell?

Dhara Pendavala's Rajkot catalogue provides a useful example of the variety available. Its published namkeen section includes garlic-flavoured sev mamra, khakhra chevdo and bhakharwadi. It shows ₹80 for a 250 g pack in that section, and ₹80 for 200 g packs of selected other snacks, including wheat chakri. The catalogue also lists a 250 g farali chevdo option at ₹90. These prices are a dated example checked on 25 November 2026, not a recommended spending amount or a market average.

Fresh breakfast or a travel pack?

For a sit-down breakfast, ask whether the shop serves fresh ganthiya with chutney, chillies or other accompaniments. If buying for a journey, look for an intact seal, a readable ingredients list and storage directions. Fresh sides such as chutney may need separate handling. Do not treat a dry-looking snack as a guarantee of safe storage.

How to compare two farsan shops fairly

Compare the same product and pack weight, rather than comparing a small premium pack to a larger plain mixture. Check whether the listed price includes packaging. Ask staff about ingredients if you have an allergy or require a particular preparation. Farali or vegetarian descriptions do not establish that a product is free of allergens.

For first-time visitors, a selection of different textures is more informative than buying several versions of the same snack. Our breakfast guide covers the fresh morning-food experience separately.
