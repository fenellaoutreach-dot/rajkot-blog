Ramakrishna Ashrama Rajkot: History, Temple Timings and Visitor Guide

Visit the Ramakrishna Ashrama on Dr. Yagnik Road, with published temple hours, history, library details and transport references.

Shri Ramakrishna Ashrama on Dr. Yagnik Road offers visitors a different perspective on Rajkot's cultural life. It is a working religious and community institution, not simply a tourist monument. The campus welcomes visitors for worship and contemplation, while its wider activities include education, publications and public service.

History and significance

The Ramakrishna Math's central organisation at Belur Math records the Rajkot centre's establishment in 1927. Its religious tradition is associated with Sri Ramakrishna, Sarada Devi and Swami Vivekananda. Visitors interested in the history of the city can consider how such institutions connect religious practice with education and community welfare.

What to see and learn

The temple's architecture and quiet grounds are the main attractions for many short visits. The national tourism page draws attention to the building's domes and ornamental features. Visitors can take time to appreciate these details without interrupting worship. Belur Math's centre profile also describes a public library, a publication department and educational activities for children and young people. Check membership and access requirements before planning a library visit.

Location and how to reach

The official visitor page gives the address as Dr. Yagnik Road, Rajkot 360001. It lists the campus as 2.9 km from Rajkot Junction, about half a kilometre from Bhaktinagar station, and 30.2 km from Hirasar airport. These are the institution's published reference distances rather than live route calculations.

For current details, the ashrama lists 0281-2465200 and rajkot@rkmm.org. Confirm opening arrangements before travelling from outside the city.

Plan a respectful visit

Keep voices low near worship and meditation areas, follow signs about footwear and photography, and avoid entering restricted offices or service areas. If visiting with children, explain that some parts of the campus are used for quiet religious activities. Ask staff about accessibility when planning a visit for someone who needs step-free access.

Combine with other city attractions

The Rotary Dolls Museum and Watson Museum provide complementary cultural experiences in Rajkot. Select one or two additional stops based on their confirmed schedules rather than trying to cover every attraction in a single afternoon.
