Rajkot Birth and Death Certificates: RMC Download and Correction Guide

Find Rajkot's official birth and death certificate services, prepare record details, distinguish a certificate copy from a correction and avoid unofficial agents.

Birth and death certificates are official civil registration records, not ordinary identity documents that a third-party website can issue. Rajkot Municipal Corporation lists certificate-related services and forms for events registered within its jurisdiction. This guide helps families identify the correct starting point for a copy, a correction or an unresolved registration.

Start with the authority that registered the event

Determine where the birth or death was registered. Living in Rajkot does not necessarily mean that the original event is in RMC's register, particularly if it occurred in a different municipality or district. Use the registering authority's official service rather than paying an intermediary who promises to locate every record.

How to look for a certificate copy

  1. Visit RMC's official website or the official RMC Citizen App. The published app description includes birth and death certificate downloads.
  2. Choose the service corresponding to the event. Keep the registration reference, date, names and place details available if known. Follow the fields actually requested by the official interface.
  3. Review the displayed record carefully before downloading. Confirm spelling, dates and identifying details against the original information.
  4. If the record cannot be located, ask the registering office whether it requires a manual search or additional information. An unsuccessful online search does not establish that the event was never registered.

Copy, correction or name addition?

RMC's forms directory distinguishes requests for certificate copies from changes to birth/death details and adding a child's name. The municipality also lists separate birth and death reporting forms. Select the process that matches your situation; downloading an existing record does not amend it.

Prepare for an office visit

Keep the existing certificate or registration reference if available, the event date and place, and details of the requested correction. Ask the registering office what supporting documents, authorization, fees and application route are required for your particular case. The RMC office directory can help you find municipal facilities, but confirm that the chosen office handles the relevant service.

Protect the record

Do not post a full certificate, identity number or other private family information in a public comment or social media group. Share documents only through the authorized process. Verify any payment request against the official municipality channel.
