Regional Science Centre Rajkot: Timings, Tickets and Six Science Galleries

Plan a family or school visit to Regional Science Centre Rajkot with published opening hours, admission notes and gallery highlights.

Regional Science Centre (RSC) is a worthwhile addition to a Rajkot itinerary for families interested in hands-on learning. India's Ministry of Tourism dates its establishment to 2022, while Gujarat Tourism describes a ten-acre campus with six themed galleries. Unlike a traditional museum focused primarily on historical objects, the centre introduces scientific ideas through exhibits and educational activities.

Opening hours and admission

The Incredible India visitor page lists 10 am to 6 pm, with Mondays closed. Its admission information is less clear: the page mentions ₹10 for school groups of at least ten, ₹20 for general visitors above three years of age, and ₹20 for weekends and public holidays. A separate line also lists ₹50 without clearly identifying the associated admission category. These figures are not a reliable consolidated price list. Confirm the applicable entry rate, any separate gallery or activity fees, and the final admission time before travelling.

Planning a family visit

Allow a few hours rather than rushing through all six galleries. Choose two or three subjects that interest your group most, then explore the others as time permits. Younger children may enjoy discussing what they observe rather than reading every display. Follow the venue's instructions about which exhibits can be touched. Check accessibility, refreshments and photography rules in advance if they matter to your group.

School trips and educational programmes

The Ministry of Tourism lists school visits and educational activities among the centre's facilities. Teachers should ask about advance booking, group capacity, available sessions, accompanying adults and fees. A useful classroom follow-up is to ask students to explain one exhibit in their own words.
