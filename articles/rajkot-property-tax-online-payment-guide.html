Rajkot Property Tax: How to Check, Pay and Save Your RMC Receipt

Use Rajkot's official property tax lookup, receipt and assessment portals; compare old and new property numbers and prepare correction requests.

A property tax payment in Rajkot starts with identifying the correct municipal property record. The Rajkot Municipal Corporation (RMC) website provides separate facilities for looking up property tax, making a payment and accessing bills or receipts. This guide explains how to use those official entry points without relying on unofficial payment agents.

Before opening the payment portal

Keep your latest municipal tax bill or previous receipt ready. Note the property identifier as it appears on that record, the address and the name associated with the assessment. If you own several properties, distinguish each record before making any payment. A utility consumer number or a postal address alone may not identify the correct municipal tax account.

How to check and pay Rajkot property tax online

  1. Open RMC's official website and select the property tax section. For payments, use the property tax option on the municipal payment services page.
  2. Use the property tax lookup to identify the account. The published form includes an old-property-tax option; follow the current fields rather than assuming every assessment uses the same identifier format.
  3. Compare the address and demand amount with your own records. Check whether the displayed total includes previous dues or adjustments.
  4. Proceed using the payment option offered by the official service. Save the transaction reference and retrieve the municipal receipt when available.

Which RMC property tax form should you use?

RMC maintains separate online tools for viewing an assessment and retrieving payment records. Both the tax view and bill and receipt forms accept old or new property identifiers. The newer identifier is displayed in a four-digit, four-digit, three-digit pattern. Copy the identifier from your own record, including leading zeros, rather than guessing it from your address.

Prepare an assessment application before uploading documents

The online assessment form asks for owner and occupier details, property address, mobile number, use category, ward, locality, age or completion date, carpet area, usage subtype and occupancy type. It also provides a reference-document upload and CAPTCHA. Gather accurate information before starting, particularly where the occupier and owner are different. Do not substitute built-up area for carpet area without checking the applicable definition. The form alone does not establish which supporting document is sufficient in every case, nor does submission guarantee approval. Confirm document requirements with RMC and retain any acknowledgement. Never share your property records or payment credentials with this independent website.

When a bill looks wrong

Payment does not automatically correct an outdated owner name or an incorrect assessment. RMC's forms menu separately lists assessment-copy or objection requests, name transfer and self-assessment. Identify which process applies before submitting documents. Ask the responsible municipal office about evidence, deadlines and any applicable fees; this guide does not assume a fixed correction charge or processing time.

What to do if a payment is debited but the receipt is missing

Check the official bill or receipt facility and retain the bank transaction reference. Avoid immediately paying the same demand again. If the municipality has not recorded the transaction, ask the official support channel how to reconcile it. Keep the eventual municipal receipt alongside your bank record.

Are discounts or deadlines fixed?

No rate or rebate is quoted here because municipal assessment and payment conditions may change. Check the current bill and the latest official notice for the relevant financial year. A past rebate announcement is not proof of a current entitlement.
