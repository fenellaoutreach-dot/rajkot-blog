How to apply: GCAS and ACPC admission portals

  • Gujarat Common Admission Services (GCAS): check participating institutions, programme choices and current circulars for university and college admissions. Saurashtra University's GCAS information is a useful starting point.
  • Admission Committee for Professional Courses (ACPC): consult current professional-course admission and counselling notices for the relevant programme.

Not every course uses the same admission process. Confirm the portal for your course and academic year with the institution; this page does not claim applications are currently open.

How to Choose a College in Rajkot

  1. Start with the course: Compare the actual curriculum, electives, practical training and academic requirements.
  2. Check recognition: Confirm the institution's current status and any course-specific approvals with official regulators and the college.
  3. Understand total costs: Ask for a written breakdown of tuition, examination, transport, hostel and other charges.
  4. Review location and facilities: Consider commute, libraries, laboratories, accessibility and student support.
  5. Compare outcomes carefully: Ask for programme-specific placement information, internship support and recent evidence rather than relying on broad marketing claims.

Admissions: A Practical Checklist

Admission procedures differ by institution and programme. Some courses may use entrance examinations or centralised counselling, while others follow institution-specific processes. Always use the current official admission notice.

  • Identify your preferred course and check minimum qualifications.
  • Confirm application dates, examination requirements and counselling rules.
  • Prepare academic records and identification documents requested by the institution.
  • Check scholarship and financial-aid eligibility from official sources.
  • Keep copies of application confirmations and payment receipts.

Questions Students Often Ask

Which is the best college in Rajkot?

There is no single best choice for everyone. Compare institutions based on your intended course, verified approvals, costs, academic environment and personal priorities.

Which colleges in Rajkot offer engineering or science?

Government Engineering College is a starting point for engineering, and H & H B Kotak Institute of Science for science. The universities listed above offer additional options; compare current programme lists before applying.

How can I find current admission dates?

Use each university's official admissions website or contact its admissions office. Deadlines may vary by programme and academic year.

Are scholarships available?

Eligibility and availability vary. Ask institutions about their current scholarships and review applicable government scholarship portals for official requirements.
