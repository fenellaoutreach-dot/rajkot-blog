Ishwariya Park Rajkot: Picnic, Nature and Family Visit Guide

Ishwariya Park, also spelled Iswariya Park in some listings, is a recreation and picnic destination near Rajkot. The district tourism website lists it near Madhapar on Jamnagar Road.

Where to find the park

The Rajkot district administration identifies the park as being near Madhapar, Jamnagar Road. Check the exact entrance and local route before setting off, especially if travelling by public transport.

Why families visit

The official tourism listing describes Ishwariya Park as a picnic spot with a pleasant atmosphere. It can be a flexible option for an unhurried outdoor break rather than a tightly scheduled sightseeing stop.

A relaxed visit plan

Consider walking, sitting outdoors and spending time with family or friends where permitted. Follow posted park rules and keep paths clear for other visitors.

What to carry

Water, sun protection and comfortable footwear are sensible for an outdoor outing. Check whether food, picnic mats or particular activities are allowed before bringing them.

Check facilities before visiting

Opening hours, entry charges, toilets, parking and other facilities may change. Verify current arrangements through official or on-site information.

Keep the park clean

Use waste bins, avoid disturbing plants or wildlife and leave the space in good condition for other visitors.
