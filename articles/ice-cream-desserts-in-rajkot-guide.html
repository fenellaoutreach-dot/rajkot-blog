Ice Cream in Rajkot: Historic Parlours, Shakes and Dessert Guide

Explore Rajkot's historic Satya Vijay Patel Ice Cream and Creamzen's published dessert menus, with branch information and tips for choosing ice cream, kulfi and shakes.

Rajkot's dessert culture offers two rather different experiences: traditional milk-based ice cream associated with long-established local businesses and contemporary dessert shops serving shakes, waffles and elaborate ice cream combinations. A short dessert outing can fit between a shopping trip and dinner, or become a separate evening stop.

This guide focuses on two businesses for which published information is available. It is not an exhaustive directory, a paid placement or a personal tasting ranking.

Shri Satya Vijay Patel Ice Cream: a century of local food history

A 2023 Indian Express feature traces the business to Mavji Jeram Patel in 1913 and describes its beginnings as a tea stall. The newspaper identifies it as the city's first ice cream parlour. That historical reporting is particularly valuable because it connects the shop with the development of Rajkot's food culture rather than just a recent customer rating.

The business's own website lists ice cream, thick shakes and milk-based drinks served with ice cream. It refers to Sir Lakhajiraj Road, Omkar Tower and Crescent Apartment branches and gives a contact address at Crescent B near the A.G. Office on Race Course Ring Road. Confirm the specific branch and current hours before leaving; the opening-hours wording on the site is ambiguous.

Creamzen: ice cream, cold drinks and waffles

Creamzen's published menu identifies outlets in the Nana Mava, Mota Mava, Sadhu Vasvani Road, Dr. Yagnik Road and Race Course areas. The menu includes ice cream portions, thick shakes, cold coco drinks and waffles. A Yagnik Road delivery listing also documents the business. The menu PDF is an older published document, not a live price feed; its current in-store availability and charges must be confirmed separately.

Planning a dessert stop around Rajkot

For a central-city outing, explore the heritage context of Sir Lakhajiraj Road and check the old parlour's current location. If your plans are around Race Course or Yagnik Road, the documented branch information offers a starting point for choosing a nearby dessert shop. For a group outing, confirm seating directly instead of assuming all branches have the same layout.

What to check before buying

Ask about portion size, extra toppings and the final price. For packed products, check the label and storage directions. When allergies or dietary requirements matter, ask about milk, nuts, wheat, egg-containing accompaniments and shared equipment. Do not assume that a flavour name establishes its ingredients or that a dairy-free option is suitable for every allergy.
