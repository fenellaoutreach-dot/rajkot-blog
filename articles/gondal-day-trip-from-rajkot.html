Gondal Day Trip from Rajkot: Naulakha Palace, Royal Cars and Heritage

Explore Gondal's palace architecture and vintage car collection on a flexible heritage day trip from Rajkot.

Gondal offers a change of scene for visitors who have already explored central Rajkot. Gujarat Tourism places the town about 40 km from Rajkot by road. Its former royal residences and collection of classic cars make it especially interesting for people who enjoy architecture, design and the history of transport.

Naulakha Palace: start with the architecture

Also encountered under the spelling Navlakha, Naulakha Palace is a prominent heritage stop in Gondal. The building's stone carving, layered balconies and arcades reward a close look. Gujarat Tourism includes the palace in its heritage itineraries. Check which rooms and galleries are currently accessible to day visitors rather than assuming every part of the property is open. Ask whether a guide is available and whether photography inside is permitted.

Royal Vintage and Classic Car Collection

The state tourism page describes a collection of 32 vehicles, including a 1907 New Engine Company car and a Mercedes saloon dating from 1935. These examples offer a useful contrast between early motoring and later luxury automobiles. Visitors interested in design can look for differences in bodywork, passenger seating and the use of materials across generations. The collection may change, so do not assume any particular vehicle is on display on your chosen date.

Orchard Palace and Riverside Palace

Orchard Palace was originally constructed as an extension of the Huzoor Palace residence for royal guests, according to Gujarat Tourism. Its heritage hotel listing also highlights a royal railway saloon. Riverside Palace is another heritage accommodation in the town. Both are operating hospitality properties; guest access and public sightseeing access should be treated separately. Contact the property about visits, meals or accommodation before planning around it.

Tickets, transport and practical details

The cited tourism attraction pages do not establish a current combined ticket price or a complete opening-hours schedule. Confirm each venue separately and check current rail or bus services if travelling without a car. Travellers with limited mobility should ask about stairs, uneven surfaces and accessible toilets at heritage properties. A full day is more comfortable than combining several distant attractions without time to spare.
