Unlimited Food in Rajkot: Gujarati Thali Restaurants, Prices and Dining Guide

Compare documented unlimited Gujarati thali options in Rajkot, including The Grand Atmij and Padharo, with menu details and practical ordering tips.

Looking for unlimited food in Rajkot? Start with the city's Gujarati thali restaurants, where a set-price meal may include refills of selected dishes. The important detail is what each restaurant actually includes: sweets, beverages and special items can be priced separately. Here are three places with published menu evidence, arranged by source clarity rather than taste or popularity.

The Grand Atmij: compare fixed and unlimited meals

The hotel's restaurant page lists two Gujarati thalis. Its fixed option is ₹120, with a ₹130 parcel option, whereas its unlimited option is ₹180. The unlimited menu describes four vegetable preparations, several traditional breads, buttermilk, salad and papad. It distinguishes afternoon dal-rice from evening kadhi-khichdi and lists sweets separately. The address is Panchnath Main Road opposite Maheshwari Eye Hospital, near Gandhi Museum Chowk. Check the current menu and whether the quoted price includes all applicable charges.

Padharo Restaurant: a station-area option

Padharo advertises unlimited vegetarian Gujarati thalis on its own website and also describes a banquet facility. Its contact section identifies the location opposite the railway station entry gate in Junction Plot. Because a separate part of the page contains contradictory template information, verify the location and current service hours directly rather than relying on the booking form. A current per-person price was not established by the page reviewed.

Harbhole Food and Restaurant: confirm the dining offer

Harbhole's online delivery listing identifies the restaurant on Gangotri Park Main Road, opposite Palm Universe in Raiya Village. Search results for its menu mention unlimited Gujarati thali, but a delivery listing is not sufficient evidence of an active unlimited dine-in offer. Contact the restaurant before travelling specifically for that service. The menu also covers other vegetarian and North Indian dishes.

What to ask before choosing an unlimited meal

  • Which dishes are refillable, and are sweets or drinks extra?
  • Does the offer apply at lunch, dinner or both?
  • Is the price per person, and are taxes included?
  • Are there separate rules for groups or children?
  • Can the kitchen accommodate allergies or other dietary requirements?

Fixed thali, unlimited service or buffet?

A fixed thali has a specified set of servings. Unlimited service usually means refills of selected items at the table. A buffet may instead involve a separate self-service arrangement. The names alone do not establish the conditions. Choose a format that suits your preferences and ask about any extra charges before ordering.
