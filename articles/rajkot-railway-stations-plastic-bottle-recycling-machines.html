Rajkot and Bhaktinagar Railway Stations Get Plastic Bottle Crushing Machines

Western Railway has installed bottle crushing facilities at Rajkot, Bhaktinagar and three other stations, according to a November 26 report.

Rail passengers in Rajkot have a new option for disposing of used plastic drink bottles. Gujarat Samachar reported on Thursday that Western Railway has added bottle compacting equipment at Ahmedabad and four locations within its Rajkot Division.

Which railway stations are covered?

The report identifies Rajkot, Bhaktinagar, Jamnagar and Dwarka as the four divisional stations receiving one machine each through a corporate social responsibility initiative. Ahmedabad is the fifth station named in the report.

Why the machines matter

Reducing the volume of discarded bottles can make their collection and movement more manageable. The equipment does not, by itself, establish that all material collected will be recycled. Effective sorting, collection and downstream processing remain important parts of the waste-management chain.

In a separate July 2026 statement, the Ministry of Railways described bottle crushing equipment as one element of its wider waste management measures, alongside treatment and material recovery facilities at selected railway premises.

What passengers should know

Look for designated waste collection points and follow the instructions posted at the station. Do not leave rubbish beside equipment that is full or unavailable; use the appropriate designated bins instead. Ask station personnel if you are unsure where to dispose of an item.

What has yet to be confirmed

The November report indicates that Western Railway intends to extend the initiative. It does not provide a station-by-station expansion timetable, the precise platform locations of the machines, operating capacity or verified recycling volumes. Rajkot.blog has not independently inspected the installations.
