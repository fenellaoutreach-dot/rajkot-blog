Rajkot Bus & Travel Operators: Official Booking and Passenger Guide

Find official GSRTC and private bus booking websites serving Rajkot, compare documented routes and check boarding points, tickets and refunds before travel.

Rajkot passengers can use the state road transport corporation or private coach operators. The right choice depends on the destination, boarding point and ticket conditions, not simply the advertised fare. This independent directory is an initial selection of operators with identifiable first-party websites, not a complete list of every travel agency in the city.

GSRTC: booking and ticket problems

The GSRTC portal provides ticket retrieval, cancellation, journey rescheduling and refund enquiry functions. Its published customer support number is 1800 233 666666. Enter your own correct contact details when booking so that you can retrieve the reservation later. If money is debited but no ticket appears, use the transaction enquiry before making another payment.

Private coaches: compare the destination before paying

For Ahmedabad, both Sudama and Mahasagar list city services and separate airport destinations. Do not assume a city drop includes the airport. Sudama also lists Porbandar in both directions, making its own website a useful starting point for a return journey. Jay Somnath identifies its office at Sahyog Complex behind the old bus stand; this is an office address, not a confirmed boarding point for every departure. Samay's contact page lists its Rajkot office at Limda Chowk and Ahmedabad branches including Paldi and Naroda Bethak. Confirm the actual pickup and drop on your ticket rather than treating branch addresses as stops.

Before you leave for the bus

  1. Search the exact travel date and direction on the operator website. Check whether a late-night departure falls on the following calendar day.
  2. Compare the final payable amount, seat or berth type, luggage rules and cancellation terms.
  3. Save the ticket and the operator's current contact details. Check the pickup landmark and reporting time on the reservation.
  4. Allow time for the journey to the pickup point. Private coach offices and the central bus stand are not interchangeable.
  5. For a connecting flight or train, leave a realistic buffer and check the actual drop location.
