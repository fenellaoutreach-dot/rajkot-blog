Kathiyawadi Dinner in Rajkot: Rotlo, Sev Tameta and Traditional Sides

A practical introduction to ordering a traditional Kathiyawadi dinner in Rajkot.

Start with bajra rotlo

Rotlo is a thick millet flatbread commonly associated with Kathiyawadi cooking. Pair it with a vegetable preparation, dal or curd for a varied meal. Ask whether ghee is served separately if you prefer to choose your own accompaniments.

Try sev tameta

This tomato-based preparation combines a tangy gravy with crunchy gram-flour sev. Texture and spice levels vary between kitchens. Ask whether the sev is added just before serving.

Explore traditional sides

Garlic chutney, pickles, jaggery and chaas are familiar accompaniments. You may also find ringan no olo, a roasted eggplant preparation. Menus differ, so do not assume every restaurant serves each dish.

Ordering a meal

Ask what is included in a fixed thali, whether refills are available and whether individual dishes can be ordered separately. For food allergies, confirm ingredients directly with staff, particularly dairy, wheat and chickpea flour. Check current prices before ordering.
