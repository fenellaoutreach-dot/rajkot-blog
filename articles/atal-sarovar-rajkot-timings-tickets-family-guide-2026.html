Atal Sarovar at a glance

Atal Sarovar is a landscaped waterfront recreation destination associated with the Rajkot Smart City development programme. Its official website describes a park of more than 75 acres with recreational attractions and a food court. It is a useful option for families and visitors who want an evening outing rather than a conventional heritage stop. Source: official park overview.

Opening hours and weekly closure

The official website currently lists Tuesday to Sunday opening hours of 4:00 PM to 11:00 PM, with ticket counter and entry closing at 10:30 PM. It says the park is closed every Monday, with the Monday closure in effect from February 1, 2026. Check the official website on the day of travel, especially around public holidays or maintenance. Official timings.

Entry tickets and attraction charges

The official booking page has displayed a basic entry ticket at ₹30 per person, but ticketing options and availability can change. The same page has also shown separate higher-priced ride packages, so do not assume a basic entry ticket includes rides. Confirm the current price, age rules, ride inclusions and whether tickets are sold online or at the counter before travelling. Official ticket and package page.

Where is Atal Sarovar?

The official contact page identifies the site in the New Raiya Smart City area, near New 150-ft Ring Road, Rajkot. The park website also uses the address 'Race Course 8, Rajkot'. These are official descriptions of the location, not two separate attractions. Confirm your navigation pin before leaving. Official contact and location.

Rides, lakeside views and food

The park advertises rides and attractions including a Ferris wheel, alongside a waterfront setting and a food court. Its website refers to more than 15 food court options; treat this as a description from the operator rather than a verified current stall count. Individual rides may have height, age, safety, weather or operating restrictions. Attractions · Food court.

A practical two-to-three-hour evening plan

Arrive after the published 4 PM opening, allow time for a relaxed walk and photographs, then decide whether any rides fit your group and budget. Leave time for food and an unhurried return journey. This is a suggested itinerary, not an official programme or promise that every attraction will operate.

What families should check before visiting

Carry drinking water if permitted under current park rules, comfortable footwear and a charged phone. Check the weather and any temporary closures, confirm accessibility needs directly with the operator, and supervise children around water and ride areas. For groups, ask about parking and the busiest periods rather than assuming a quiet visit.

Frequently asked questions

Is Atal Sarovar open on Mondays?

The official website says it is closed every Monday. Verify any exceptional opening or closure before travelling.

What are the opening hours?

The official website lists 4 PM–11 PM Tuesday through Sunday, with last entry at 10:30 PM.

Does the ₹30 ticket include rides?

Do not assume so. The operator lists separate ticket and ride-package options; check the current inclusions.

Is Atal Sarovar suitable for a family outing?

The operator presents it as a family recreation destination. Check ride restrictions, accessibility and your group's needs directly.
