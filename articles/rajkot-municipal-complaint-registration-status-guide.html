RMC Complaint Registration in Rajkot: How to Report and Track Civic Issues

Learn where to register a municipal complaint in Rajkot, what details to prepare, how to use the official complaint status service and when to contact another authority.

A broken streetlight, missed waste collection or public drainage problem can be difficult to resolve if the complaint does not reach the correct municipal team. Rajkot Municipal Corporation provides a public complaint registration form and a separate status lookup. The following steps help residents prepare a report that can be located and followed up.

What RMC's complaint form asks for

The official registration page lists fields for the complainant's name, issue type, area, address, mobile number, phone number, pole number where relevant, remarks and a CAPTCHA. Use the live form's requirements and only provide personal details through the official channel. The page also lists its 24-hour call centre number as 2450077; check the website for the current dialing instructions.

Write a useful location description

Examples of helpful information for municipal complaints
IssueUseful details to prepare
StreetlightStreet, nearby landmark, pole identifier if safely visible
Missed waste collectionArea, date and usual collection point
Public drainage problemExact affected stretch, visible conditions and duration
Road maintenanceRoad name, direction and nearby landmark

These are reporting suggestions, not an exhaustive list of categories available in the form. Avoid entering unsafe areas to collect a photo or identifier.

Submit and track the complaint

  1. Open the official form and select the closest applicable type and area.
  2. Write a short factual description. Include when the problem began and whether it is recurring.
  3. Submit through the official service and save whatever acknowledgement or reference is provided.
  4. Use the complaint status page for updates. Keep the reference for follow-up.

When a complaint remains open

Record the dates of your previous reports and any responses. Contact the municipality through its published channels with the existing reference instead of repeatedly submitting identical complaints. The office directory helps identify local municipal facilities. We do not promise a particular response or resolution deadline.

Use the correct authority

RMC documents municipal responsibilities including roads, streetlights, water, drainage and waste management. A household electricity billing or distribution problem belongs with the electricity supplier; see the PGVCL guide. For immediate danger contact the appropriate emergency service rather than relying on an ordinary complaint ticket.
