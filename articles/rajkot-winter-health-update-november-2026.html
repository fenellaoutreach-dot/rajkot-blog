Rajkot Health Update: 1,634 Cold, Cough and Fever Cases Reported in a Week

Municipal health figures for November 16–22 show 874 cold and cough cases and 760 fever cases.

What the weekly figures show

According to an IANS report citing Rajkot Municipal Corporation figures released on Monday, the reporting week of November 16–22 included 874 cold and cough cases and 760 common fever cases. These are reported case totals, not necessarily distinct individuals.

Other illnesses in the report

The report also lists 204 cases of diarrhoea and vomiting, one typhoid case and five dengue cases. These categories should not be interpreted as diagnoses for any individual reader.

Why the update matters

The weekly figures provide a snapshot of reported illness as winter begins. They do not establish the cause of every illness or a comparison with the previous week. Residents who are concerned about symptoms should seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

What remains unknown

The cited report does not establish how many cases required hospital admission. Rajkot.blog will distinguish subsequent official updates from these reporting-period figures.
