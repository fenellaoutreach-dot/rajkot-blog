Rajkot Water Bill: Online Payment, Receipts and Connection Guide

Use official Rajkot Municipal Corporation water charge services, check your bill and payment history, and find the correct route for connection or repair requests.

For a household connected to Rajkot Municipal Corporation's water network, the municipal water charge account is the starting point for billing questions. RMC lists online payment, charge lookup and bill or receipt access separately from property tax. Use the relevant water service even if you normally pay both municipal charges together.

Check your water account before paying

Find a previous water bill or receipt and keep its connection details available. Compare the account address and any identifier displayed by the official system with the paper record. A landlord, housing society or shared connection may have a different billing arrangement from an individually registered household. Ask the responsible account holder before paying an unfamiliar demand.

Where to pay RMC water charges

  1. Start at the municipal website and locate its water-charge services. The official payments directory has a dedicated water option.
  2. Use the view or lookup service to review outstanding charges. Check the property or connection details and the billing period.
  3. Follow the current payment instructions in the official portal; save the transaction reference and municipal receipt.
  4. Use the bill and receipt section to reconcile your payment. RMC's menu describes its online water bill and receipt records as covering 2005 onward; availability for a specific account may differ.

New connection or repair: a different process

The RMC forms menu lists a water connection form and a separate connection repair form. Paying outstanding charges does not itself apply for a new connection or arrange a repair. Ask the appropriate municipal office about documentation, eligibility, inspection and applicable charges before proceeding. We have not established a universal fee or completion timeframe.

What if your water bill seems unusually high?

Compare the billing period and previous balance with earlier records. Ask the municipal office whether an adjustment, unpaid amount or another account issue explains the difference. Do not assume that every water connection is billed under the same measurement or tariff arrangement.

Report service problems separately

For a leak or supply concern in public infrastructure, describe the location and the nature of the issue to RMC. Use the municipal complaint guide for reporting and tracking. If the problem is in privately maintained plumbing, first contact the responsible building manager or qualified repair provider.

For separate municipal payments, see Rajkot property tax and our background guide to Aji Dam and the city's water heritage.
