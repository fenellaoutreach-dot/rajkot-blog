Kaba Gandhi No Delo Rajkot: Heritage, Location and Visitor Tips

Kaba Gandhi No Delo is a historic house associated with the Gandhi family in central Rajkot. The Rajkot district administration identifies it as the residence of Karamchand Gandhi, who served as Diwan of Rajkot State.

Why this place matters

The building connects visitors with the early life of Mahatma Gandhi and the period when his family lived in Rajkot. The district's tourism page describes a pictorial presentation of Gandhi's life with captions in Gujarati and Hindi.

Where it is

The district administration lists the site at Kadiya Navline, Dharmendra Road, Lohana Para, Rajkot. It is in the older urban area, so plan for local traffic and possible walking from your drop-off point.

What to look for

Pay attention to the historic building, the displays and the wider neighbourhood rather than treating the site as a quick photo stop. Read exhibition captions carefully and avoid assuming every room is open to visitors.

Opening hours and visitor arrangements

The Rajkot Municipal Corporation's tourism booklet lists a split visiting schedule and a Monday closure, but published arrangements can change. Confirm current entry, photography rules and opening hours before travelling.

Combine it with nearby heritage

Consider pairing this visit with the Watson Museum or Mahatma Gandhi Museum, subject to current opening arrangements. Keep the schedule flexible and allow time for traffic and meals.

Respectful visiting checklist

Avoid touching historic exhibits, keep noise low, ask permission before photographing people and follow any instructions posted at the venue.
