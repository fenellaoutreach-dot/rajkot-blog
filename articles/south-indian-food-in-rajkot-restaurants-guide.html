South Indian Food in Rajkot: Dosa, Idli and Restaurant Menu Guide

Compare four documented Rajkot South Indian outlets, Sankalp's Race Course and Raiya Circle branches, menu examples and dietary ordering tips.

If you are searching for the best South Indian food in Rajkot, the useful starting point is the type of dish you want and the branch convenient to your journey. A crisp dosa, steamed idli and thick uttapam offer different experiences. This guide compares four documented outlets using their public menus and location information, not a tasting-based ranking.

22nd Parallel: explore different dosa styles

The Akashvani Chowk delivery menu lists set dosa at ₹210, Chettinad dosa at ₹270 and dosa fry at ₹280. Its dosa fry is a variation featuring a seasoned filling and additional cooking on the griddle. The set dosa option consists of smaller, thicker crepes. These prices are delivery-menu snapshots, not confirmed restaurant-table prices. A separate Ayodhya Chowk listing also exists, so check which branch you are viewing before comparing prices.

Ordering beyond masala dosa at Sankalp

The official chain menu provides useful ingredient clues: onion dosa includes garlic chutney, rava dosa uses semolina, and military ghee roast uses clarified butter. Tomato corn uttapam is described as a Jain speciality. Ask whether the Rajkot branch offers these dishes and whether accompaniments and shared preparation meet your dietary requirements. For a shared meal, the chain lists a three-variety dosa platter; confirm portion sizes and price before ordering. Semolina contains wheat, so do not assume all dosa varieties are gluten-free.

The delivery menu identifies this outlet at Hawkers Zone Food Market near A.G. Chowk, opposite Prem Mandir. Its published prices include ₹85 for sada dosa, ₹114 for masala dosa and ₹171 for mixed uttapam. Check current availability and whether on-site seating is suitable for your group. The listing is evidence of menu items, not an independent quality or hygiene assessment.

What is the difference between these dishes?

Masala dosa typically wraps a spiced potato filling inside a thin crepe. Set dosa is generally thicker, while uttapam can carry vegetables directly on its surface. Idli is steamed rather than cooked as a crepe. Sambar and chutneys are common accompaniments, although recipes vary by kitchen. Ask whether the menu includes accompaniments or charges separately for extras.

How to choose a restaurant for your visit

Check branch-specific menus and current hours, particularly if you are travelling from another part of Rajkot. Compare similar dishes rather than assuming a higher price indicates better quality. If allergies or dietary needs matter, ask about shared griddles and ingredients in chutneys, batter and toppings. Delivery prices and charges may differ from dine-in bills.
