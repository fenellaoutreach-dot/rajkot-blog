Student Life in Rajkot: Hostels, PG, Living Costs & Transport

Updated 28 November 2026 · Rajkot.blog editorial team

Students moving to Rajkot should compare campus hostels, paying-guest (PG) accommodation and shared rentals before committing. Your monthly living costs depend on rent, meals and the campus commute. Use the checklist below to collect actual quotes instead of relying on an unverified fixed budget.

Choose a college before choosing a neighbourhood

Start with your institution's official address and class schedule. A room that looks convenient on a map may still involve a difficult daily commute. Compare travel options at the times you expect to attend classes. Our Rajkot college guide covers broader admissions considerations.

Compare hostels, paying-guest rooms and rentals

Ask whether accommodation includes electricity, water, internet, meals and laundry. Understand deposits, notice periods, visitor policies and refund terms before paying. If possible, inspect the property with a trusted adult or representative and request written documentation.

Build a realistic monthly budget

Create categories for accommodation, food, commuting, mobile data, course materials, personal needs and an emergency buffer. Costs vary considerably by location and lifestyle, so use current quotes rather than assuming an online estimate is accurate.

Student monthly living cost worksheet

Collect monthly quotes in INR and separate one-time costs
ExpenseHow to calculate
Rent or hostelMonthly charge; convert term-based fees to a monthly equivalent
FoodMess fees or daily food allowance times days in the month
TransportMonthly pass or daily return fare times class days
Utilities and study needsElectricity share, data, laundry and course materials
One-time expensesDeposit, moving costs and essential room supplies (separate from monthly total)

Add recurring expenses and an emergency buffer. Check whether meals or utilities are already included to avoid double counting.

Plan daily transport

Check the route between your accommodation and campus. Consider walking distance, public transport availability and the reliability of your chosen commute. Save official transport information and keep an alternative plan for late classes or bad weather.

Rajkot Municipal Corporation lists student and general bus pass forms on its official website and provides a bus pass registration page. Confirm eligibility, routes, documents, applicable charges and current procedure with the operator. For intercity travel, see our Rajkot bus and travel operators guide.

Set up your study routine

Identify libraries, quiet study spaces and reliable internet access. Keep digital copies of important academic documents in a secure location, and note important college deadlines in a calendar.

Stay safe and informed

Share your accommodation details with a trusted contact, learn local emergency procedures, and follow your college's student-support guidance. Avoid sending deposits or identification documents to unverified listings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Rajkot suitable for students from outside Gujarat?

Rajkot has educational institutions and student accommodation options, but suitability depends on your course, budget and support needs.

How can I estimate living expenses?

Compare current accommodation quotes and create a budget covering essentials and a contingency amount.

Should I choose a PG or a college hostel?

Compare campus distance, meals, curfews, utilities, deposit and refund rules. Ask the college about official hostel availability before paying a private accommodation provider.

What should I verify before paying for a room?

Confirm the property, written terms, included services, deposits and refund conditions.

Editor's note: This is an informational guide. Details such as prices, business hours, transport services, policies and weather can change. Verify current information with relevant official or local sources before making decisions.
