Rajkot Ration Shop Relocation Approved; Civil Supplies Panel Reviews Distribution

A civil supplies advisory committee meeting in Rajkot has resulted in approval to relocate a deteriorating fair-price shop, according to a local news report published on 21 November 2026.

Loktej English reported that the Rajkot City and District Civil Supply and Consumer Protection Advisory Committee met under District Collector Dr. Om Prakash. The discussion covered food-grain storage, the availability of essential commodities, public distribution arrangements and implementation of government schemes.

Ration Shop to Move to Canal Road

According to the report, the Collector approved moving a ration shop from the Laludi Vokali area to the Hathikhana area on Canal Road because its existing premises were in poor condition.

The committee also considered a closed fair-price shop in Gondal taluka. Rather than opening a replacement shop in that locality, the reported direction was to attach the affected beneficiaries permanently to another shop.

Ujjwala Scheme Mentioned in Meeting Coverage

The same Loktej English report was published under a headline stating that 588 gas connections had been provided under the Ujjwala scheme. The figure is attributed to that report; Rajkot.blog has not independently confirmed the number or the period it covers.

What Residents Should Know

The reported decision concerns how eligible households access public distribution services. However, the source report does not establish an effective relocation date, revised shop hours or a beneficiary-by-beneficiary transition schedule. Residents affected by the change should confirm operational details with the relevant local civil supplies office before travelling.

Source and Verification

This article is an independently written summary of a report by Loktej English, published 21 November 2026. Rajkot.blog did not attend the committee meeting or obtain its minutes independently. Further updates should be checked against official district communications.

Editorial transparency: The reported decisions and figure above are attributed to Loktej English. No independent on-site verification or official order review is claimed.
