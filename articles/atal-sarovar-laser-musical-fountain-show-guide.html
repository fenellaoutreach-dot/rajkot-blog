Atal Sarovar Laser & Musical Fountain Show: Timings and Ticket Checks

Rajkot.blog editorial team · 28 November 2026

Atal Sarovar in Rajkot has an officially documented musical fountain, and its project designer describes a laser-equipped installation. However, a current show start time or daily performance schedule could not be confirmed from the official attraction or ticketing pages on 28 November 2026. Do not confuse the park's opening hours with the show schedule.

How to check before leaving home

The official contact page lists +91 63596 03989. Ask whether the fountain and laser effects are operating on your visit date, the exact start time, whether a separate ticket is needed, where viewing is permitted, and whether weather or maintenance can change the programme. The official gallery contains venue imagery; use it rather than assuming an illustrative photograph represents the show.

Does the ₹30 ticket cover the fountain?

The official booking page displayed basic entry at ₹30 and described limited access without rides or attractions. It also directed visitors to offline tickets at the time of review. This does not establish whether fountain viewing is included, separately charged or temporarily unavailable. Confirm with the park rather than purchasing entry solely on the assumption that the show is included.

Plan a flexible evening

Choose your arrival after the published park opening, allow time for entry and a waterfront walk, then follow staff directions to permitted viewing areas. Leave a buffer for the return journey. If the performance is cancelled, consider the park itself only if the remaining attractions and access justify the trip. Visitors sensitive to flashing lights or loud sounds should ask the operator about the experience and keep a comfortable distance.

FAQs

Is the laser show at 7 PM or 8 PM?

Neither time was verified from the operator's current pages. Contact the park for the date-specific schedule.

Is the fountain running on Monday?

The park publishes Monday as its closure day. Do not plan a Monday visit unless an exception is confirmed by the operator.
