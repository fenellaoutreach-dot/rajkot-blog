Royal Orchid Expands Hotel Presence in Rajkot and Visakhapatnam

Recent hospitality industry coverage reports two Regenta property launches across Rajkot and Visakhapatnam.

What has been reported

A November 24 news roundup identifies recent hospitality trade coverage of Royal Orchid Hotels launching Regenta properties in Rajkot and Visakhapatnam. The roundup references Hotelier India coverage dated November 19 and ET TravelWorld coverage dated November 23.

Understanding the room figure

The roundup attributes a combined addition of 102 rooms across both cities to the ET TravelWorld report. That number should not be read as the capacity of the Rajkot property alone.

Local relevance

Additional hotel options may be relevant to business visitors and families planning trips to Rajkot. The available roundup does not establish current room rates, booking availability, employment figures or specific amenities.

Before booking

Travellers should confirm the property's exact location, opening status and booking conditions directly with the hotel. This report is not a hotel review or sponsored recommendation.

Source

Newsmriti news roundup, 24 November 2026, referring to Hotelier India and ET TravelWorld
