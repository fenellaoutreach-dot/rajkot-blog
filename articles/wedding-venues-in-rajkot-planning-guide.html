Wedding Venues in Rajkot: Resorts, Banquets and Booking Checklist

Finding a wedding venue in Rajkot involves more than choosing a beautiful space. Capacity, catering, access, weather planning and written terms all matter. This guide introduces documented venue options without claiming personal inspections or ranking them.

Regency Lagoon Resort & Convention

Public venue listings describe Regency Lagoon on Nyari Dam Road off Kalavad Road as a resort with wedding and conference facilities. Ask the property which indoor and outdoor spaces are available for your dates and party size.

Phoenix Resort

Phoenix Resort is listed near the Kalawad Road and Ishwariya Main Road corner. Its published venue materials describe restaurant, rooms, party lawns and banquet facilities. Published venue brochure (older reference). Verify all current facilities directly.

SARAZA

SARAZA describes its banquet, restaurant and event offerings on its public profiles. Check whether the banquet configuration and food service suit your event. Official links.

Jasper Café and Party Lawns

Jasper advertises party lawns and an event enquiry contact on its official website. Its Mota Mava location may be worth comparing for smaller celebrations or other events, subject to confirmed capacity. Official website.

Sayaji Hotel Rajkot

Public hotel listings identify Sayaji Rajkot as a hotel with banquet and event facilities near Kalavad Road. Request a current proposal covering space, meals, parking and accommodation if needed.

Questions to ask before paying a deposit

Request a written quote that identifies the event date, permitted hours, guest capacity, catering arrangements, décor rules, taxes, overtime charges and cancellation terms. Confirm accessibility, parking, backup power and rain contingency for outdoor functions.

Compare quotes on the same basis

A venue quote is meaningful only if it covers the same guest count, meal package, setup requirements and timing. Avoid assuming published photographs represent the exact space or configuration you will receive.

Editorial disclosure

No venue paid for inclusion and no site inspection or booking availability check was conducted. This is a starting shortlist, not a recommendation based on first-hand experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far ahead should I book?

Ask venues about date availability as early as practical, especially for popular wedding periods.

Are wedding package prices listed here?

No. Prices vary by date, guest count and services; request current written quotations.

Are the venues ranked?

No. This is a non-ranked editorial shortlist.
