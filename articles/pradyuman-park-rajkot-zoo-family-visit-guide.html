Pradyuman Park Rajkot: Zoo Visit and Family Planning Guide

Pradyuman Park is a zoological attraction on the outskirts of Rajkot. The district tourism website locates it near Marketing Yard and Lalpari Lake.

Location and what to expect

Rajkot district describes Pradyuman Park as a large zoo with animal enclosures and areas suitable for family visits. It notes a golf-cart service, but current availability should be confirmed directly.

Planning the journey

Because the park is outside the city centre, allow additional travel time and check your route before leaving. Verify parking arrangements and the last entry time.

Animal welfare and visitor etiquette

Stay behind barriers, avoid loud behaviour and never feed or disturb animals. Follow zoo staff directions and respect enclosure notices.

Weather and comfort

Outdoor attractions can be tiring during hot weather. Carry drinking water, wear comfortable shoes and check weather conditions before visiting. Take breaks when needed.

Family accessibility questions

Ask about toilets, drinking-water facilities, accessibility, stroller suitability and whether any internal transport is operating. Do not assume all paths or services are available every day.

Tickets and opening times

Entry prices, closure days and facility availability can change. Use official local information for current details rather than relying on old social-media posts.
