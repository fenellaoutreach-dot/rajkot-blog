Rooftop Solar in Rajkot: Subsidy, PGVCL and Homeowner Guide

For Rajkot homeowners, a rooftop solar decision starts with the electricity bill and the roof, not an advertised discount. This guide explains how to assess a residential installation, compare written quotations and navigate the official subsidy process without relying on sales promises.

Start here: Check the distribution company printed on your bill, collect a year of consumption records and arrange a professional roof assessment. If your connection is with PGVCL, use its official channels alongside the national solar portal.

How much central rooftop solar subsidy is available?

Under the published PM Surya Ghar residential assistance schedule, the first two kilowatts qualify for ₹30,000 per kW and the third for ₹18,000. The standard central ceiling is ₹78,000. The June 2026 government update also confirms that ceiling. These are subsidy amounts, not installation prices or guaranteed payments.

Confirm eligibility and the latest applicable amounts on the national portal before ordering. This guide does not include any unverified Gujarat state top-up. The residential scheme should not be assumed to cover a shop or factory connection.

Choose a system size using your electricity bills

Add the units consumed across twelve months and divide by twelve to obtain average monthly use. Keep the highest summer months visible separately, especially if air conditioning accounts for a large share of consumption. A household using 2,400 units annually averages 200 units a month; this is an example, not a recommendation for a particular system size.

Ask each installer to model annual output for the actual roof and explain its assumptions about shading, orientation, dust, temperature and equipment losses. Compare expected generation with consumption and the applicable export settlement rules. Do not size a system simply to maximise the subsidy.

How to apply through PM Surya Ghar and PGVCL

  1. Check the residential connection details and eligibility on the official national portal.
  2. Register using the correct distribution company and consumer number, then submit the rooftop application.
  3. Follow the portal's current approval or feasibility instructions and select an eligible registered vendor.
  4. Have the qualified vendor complete the approved installation and required documentation.
  5. Follow the utility's metering, inspection and commissioning process.
  6. Submit the required subsidy claim and bank details through official channels and track its status.

PGVCL's official contact page lists its corporate office in Laxminagar, off Nana Mava Main Road, Rajkot, and customer care numbers 19122 and 1800 233 155333. Contact the utility to confirm the process for your connection rather than assuming that a corporate-office visit is required.

What to check during a professional roof survey

Rajkot households with water tanks, parapets, neighbouring buildings or shared terraces should ask the installer to assess usable unshaded space and access. Request an assessment of roof condition, structural suitability, drainage, waterproofing and wind resistance. For apartment buildings, establish who controls the terrace and obtain any necessary permissions before paying a deposit. Leave roof access, electrical work and structural assessment to qualified adults and professionals.

Compare solar installer quotations fairly

Request at least two comparable written offers. Ask for the exact module and inverter models, rated capacity, warranty terms, mounting structure, metering and approval responsibilities, taxes, payment milestones and exclusions. Clarify who handles faults and roof damage, and whether monitoring and maintenance are included. Verify vendor registration independently rather than relying on a screenshot supplied by a salesperson.

Keep the gross contract amount separate from expected assistance. Ask how much must be paid before any subsidy arrives, and obtain a receipt for every payment. Never share banking passwords or OTPs with an installer.

Estimate savings without assuming a zero bill

Compare the expected value of solar electricity used at home and eligible export credits with recurring expenses. Fixed charges, imported electricity, seasonal variation and applicable tariff rules may leave a payable bill. A simple illustrative calculation: if net upfront expenditure is ₹90,000 and verified annual net savings are ₹15,000, simple payback is six years. These invented inputs illustrate the arithmetic only; they are not Rajkot market prices or a forecast. Financing costs and future equipment replacement can change the result.

Frequently asked questions

Will solar provide electricity during a power cut?

Ordinary grid-connected systems generally shut down during an outage for safety. Backup requires a compatible professionally designed system; ask for a separate written explanation of its capabilities and costs.

Does PM Surya Ghar guarantee 300 free units every month?

No individual generation or zero-bill outcome is guaranteed. Output and savings depend on the system, consumption, weather and billing rules.

Can a tenant or housing society apply?

Check the current ownership and connection eligibility rules, roof permissions and any separate provisions for common facilities before proceeding.

Can a Rajkot factory claim the same subsidy?

The central assistance discussed here is for residential consumers. Commercial and industrial users should obtain advice specific to their connection and applicable schemes.
