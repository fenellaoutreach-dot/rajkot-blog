Bhaktinagar Railway Station Fire in Rajkot: No Passenger Injuries Reported

Local media reports that railway personnel extinguished a fire near Bhaktinagar station. The cause remains under investigation.

What local media reported

Gujarati newspaper Sanj Samachar reported on November 24 that vegetation near the railway tracks at Bhaktinagar caught fire, prompting concern among passengers. Railway staff and Railway Protection Force personnel responded and brought the fire under control.

Passenger safety and investigation

The newspaper quoted a station public relations representative as saying that no passengers were injured. The report said the cause was being investigated. Rajkot.blog has not independently established the cause or the extent of any property damage.

Travel information

Passengers should consult official railway announcements for current service information rather than assume that the incident has caused continuing disruption.
