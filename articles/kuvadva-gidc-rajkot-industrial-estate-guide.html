Kuvadva GIDC Rajkot: Industrial Estate, Allotment Price & Factory Checks

Rajkot.blog editorial team · 28 November 2026

Kuvadva GIDC is an industrial estate listed by Gujarat Industrial Development Corporation in Rajkot district. The official FY 2026–27 allotment price schedule lists Kuvadva at ₹680 per square metre. This published rate is not a current resale quote, auction result or proof that a plot is available.

Understand what ₹680 per square metre means

For illustration only, multiplying the listed rate by 500 square metres gives ₹340,000. This is not the expected acquisition cost of an available plot: auction bids, property-specific conditions, lease costs, deposits, infrastructure and other expenses may alter the actual amount. Do not budget or transfer money based on this arithmetic example. Verify the applicable notice and current circular.

How to find an actual industrial property

Use GIDC's estate details and land bank and property inventory information. Check the exact estate, plot number, category and latest official notice. Existing lease transfers and new allotments are different processes. Consult the authority and a qualified local adviser for property-specific documentation.

Factory site visit checklist

  • Confirm surveyed plot boundaries, permitted activity, access roads and vehicle turning space.
  • Request written information on electricity load, water, drainage and waste-handling services.
  • Review lease conditions, transfer approvals, dues and maintenance responsibilities.
  • Check environmental and fire safety requirements relevant to the intended process.
  • Evaluate workforce transport, supplier access and freight costs using actual routes.

Sourcing manufacturers in Kuvadva

Start with the factory's full registered name and exact industrial address. For engineering parts, ask for process capability, material traceability, sample inspection and realistic lead times. FAQs

Can a new factory directly buy a vacant GIDC plot?

Kuvadava is listed under the saturated-estate category, whose published rule requires public auction for new industries. Check the current notice and any applicable exceptions with GIDC.

Is ₹680/m² the market value?

No. It is the published allotment rate for the stated financial year, not a verified sale price or winning bid.
