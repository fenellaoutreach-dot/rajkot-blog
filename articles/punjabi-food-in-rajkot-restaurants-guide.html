Punjabi Food in Rajkot: Thalis, Chole Bhature and Restaurant Guide

Find documented Punjabi dining options in Rajkot, including Premvatika, Mehul's Kitchen and Bali's Punjabi Dhaba, with menu and branch details.

Rajkot's dining scene extends beyond Gujarati and Kathiyawadi meals. Restaurants also serve Punjabi-style thalis, paneer curries, chole bhature and other North Indian dishes. If you are looking for the best Punjabi food in Rajkot, begin by comparing what different menus offer rather than trusting an unsupported ranking. The three establishments below have public menu or location evidence.

Premvatika: multiple branches for vegetarian meals

Premvatika's official website describes a vegetarian menu covering Gujarati and Punjabi dishes. Its Punjabi small thali includes a paneer preparation, dal, rice, roti and accompaniments; chole bhature is also highlighted. Its Ramapir branch is on 150 Feet Ring Road near Ramapir Chowk, opposite Om Petrol. The Mavdi branch is on the first floor of Silver Plaza at Mavdi Chowk, and the Metoda branch is near Aastha Gate opposite Jyoti CNC. The website supplies separate branch phone numbers and order links. Check the branch menu rather than assuming every dish is available everywhere.

Mehul's Kitchen: fixed meals and individual combinations

The Dr Yagnik Road outlet's delivery menus include Punjabi meal categories and paneer-based dishes. Swiggy identifies the location as Sarveshwar Chowk and lists a Punjabi thali with sweet alongside paneer rice bowls. These are useful options to compare when choosing between a complete meal and a single dish. Check directly if dine-in seating or takeaway packaging is important. A delivery listing does not establish the exact dining arrangement.

Bali's Punjabi Dhaba: another central-city option

District and Zomato identify Bali's Punjabi Dhaba at 15/A, Sarveshwar Chowk, Sardar Nagar, Dr Yagnik Road. Both offer menu or dining information, but current item prices were not independently confirmed for this guide. Review its latest menu or contact the establishment before travelling. Its proximity to Mehul's Kitchen makes it possible to compare central-city options without assuming either is superior.

How to read a Punjabi menu

Paneer butter masala usually features a tomato-based gravy, while other paneer dishes may use different spices and textures. Chole bhature pairs chickpea curry with fried bread. Dal, rice and rotis may be included in a thali or offered separately. Ask which breads and sides accompany a main dish. Spice levels and ingredients vary, and vegetarian descriptions do not guarantee that a dish is dairy-free or suitable for a particular allergy.

What about prices?

Menu prices depend on the branch, portion and ordering channel. Current comparable dine-in prices were not established across these three venues, so this guide does not invent a price range or name a cheapest restaurant. Check the menu for your chosen branch, including any taxes, extras and delivery charges. For group dining, confirm seating before setting out.
