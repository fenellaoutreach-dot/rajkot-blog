Editorial Policy

Understand Rajkot.blog editorial standards, sourcing, corrections and use of illustrative media.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Editorial purpose

Rajkot.blog publishes practical guides and locally relevant information for people interested in Rajkot, Gujarat. We aim to prioritize clarity, usefulness, transparency and accurate attribution.

Sourcing and verification

For reported news, we aim to identify original publishers or official sources wherever feasible. We distinguish reported claims from confirmed facts and avoid presenting third-party summaries as first-hand reporting. For evergreen guides, readers should verify details that can change.

Corrections

We aim to correct material factual errors when credible evidence is available. Corrections may involve revising an article and, where appropriate, adding a note explaining a significant change. Please provide the relevant page URL and supporting evidence through our contact page when contact service is active.

Images and attribution

Some images are selected to illustrate a topic rather than document the specific location or event. We do not intend these images to imply on-site photography or eyewitness evidence.

Independence and sponsored material

We aim to keep editorial decisions separate from advertising or commercial influence. If sponsored material is published in the future, it should be clearly identified as sponsored or advertising content.

Updates to this policy

As Rajkot.blog grows, editorial processes may evolve. We will update this page to reflect material changes.
