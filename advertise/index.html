Advertise With Rajkot.blog

Rajkot.blog is an independent, growing digital publication covering local news, food, travel, education and business in Rajkot, Gujarat. We welcome relevant enquiries from local businesses and organizations interested in reaching readers exploring the city.

Ways to work with us

  • Sponsored business profiles: Clearly labelled articles introducing a business, its services and useful verified details.
  • Sponsored guides and features: Paid collaborations on relevant local topics, identified as advertising or sponsored content.
  • Display advertising: Enquire about future banner and placement opportunities, subject to availability.
  • Corrections and editorial tips: Send accurate business updates or local story leads for independent editorial consideration, without any promise of coverage.

Our editorial standards

Payment does not buy positive reviews, editorial rankings, guaranteed search positions or undisclosed endorsements. Sponsored material will be clearly identified, and paid outbound links will use appropriate sponsorship attributes. We may decline content that is misleading, unverifiable or unsuitable for our audience.

Audience and pricing

We are an early-stage publication. Verified traffic figures and a rate card are not currently published. We will discuss suitable formats, timelines and pricing individually, without promising views, clicks, leads or sales.

Contact for partnerships

Email editor@rajkot.blog with your business name, website, preferred collaboration and budget range. Please include any relevant factual details for verification.
