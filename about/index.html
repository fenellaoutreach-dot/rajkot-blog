About Us

Learn about Rajkot.blog, our mission, coverage and approach to independent Rajkot city guides.

Our mission

Rajkot.blog is an independent city information website focused on Rajkot, Gujarat. We aim to make useful local information easier to discover through readable guides and clearly attributed updates.

What we cover

Our topics include local news, travel, food, education and business. Some articles are evergreen city guides; others summarize developments reported by identified sources.

How we work

We are a developing publication. We do not claim to have visited every featured place, personally interviewed sources, or independently verified every event. Where information comes from external reporting, we aim to identify that reporting and distinguish it from original reporting.

Some images are illustrative and may not depict the exact place or event discussed. Readers should confirm changing details, such as admissions, opening hours, fees and government procedures, with official sources.

Corrections and feedback

If you notice an error, please use the contact details on our Contact Us page. We welcome specific corrections and supporting references.
