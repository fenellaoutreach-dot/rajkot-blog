Last updated: 22 November 2026

Overview

Rajkot.blog is a public information website. This policy explains the data that may be processed when you visit the site. We do not currently provide user accounts, comment forms or a newsletter signup.

Our hosting provider and internet infrastructure may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, request URLs, timestamps and security-related data to deliver and protect the website. The precise retention and handling of that data depend on the provider's settings and policies.

Third-party content

Some articles use images served from Unsplash image infrastructure. When your browser loads these images, the image provider may receive technical request information under its own privacy practices.

Cookies and analytics

We have not intentionally added advertising trackers or an independent analytics script to the current static website. Hosting and security services may nevertheless use technical mechanisms necessary for delivery or protection. If analytics, advertising or other services are introduced, this policy should be updated before or alongside their use.

External links

Links to third-party websites are governed by those websites' own terms and privacy policies. We are not responsible for their data practices.

Children's privacy

The website is intended for general audiences and does not intentionally solicit personal information from children.

Changes and questions

We may update this policy as our website and services change. For contact details and their current availability, see Contact Us.
